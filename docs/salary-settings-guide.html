Salary Settings
Explained with examples

A practical guide to the four shared calculation rules in Settings → Salary Settings.

These settings apply to all employees and outlets. Each employee’s salary amounts, shift, leave entitlements and holidays still come from their own contract. The figures below are illustrative.

1. How should monthly salary be divided?

This determines the daily salary rate used for proration and unpaid-time deductions.

Example: Monthly salary of RM 3,000, with 30 calendar days and 22 scheduled working days in the month.
ChoiceCalculationDaily rate
Calendar daysRM 3,000 ÷ 30RM 100.00
Scheduled working daysRM 3,000 ÷ 22About RM 136.36

One unpaid working day therefore deducts RM 100.00 or approximately RM 136.36, depending on the selected rule.

With the working-day method, weekly offs and holidays are excluded from the divisor. A fully attended month still earns the full monthly salary; those days do not add extra pay.

2. How should attendance deductions be calculated?

Choose whether payment follows the attendance status or actual recorded working hours.

Example: A daily salary amount of RM 100 and a contract requiring 8 working hours after breaks.
AttendanceAttendance statusWorked hours
Present; worked 8 hoursRM 100 payableRM 100 payable
Present; worked 6 hoursRM 100 payableRM 75 payable
Half-day; worked 4 hoursRM 50 payableRM 50 payable

Paid fraction = 6 ÷ 8 = 75% Payable amount = RM 100 × 75% = RM 75 Deduction = RM 25

Recorded breaks are subtracted before calculating worked time. Missing or invalid punches require review. Use Worked hours if shorter working time should reduce salary proportionally.

Understanding deductions

How the shared policy determines percentage bases and fixed-deduction proration.

3. What should percentage deductions be based on?

This determines the amount used when a contract deduction is a percentage.

Example after attendance adjustments:
Basic salary: RM 2,700
Contract allowances: RM 540
Total contract earnings: RM 3,240
Percentage deduction: 10%
ChoiceCalculationDeduction
Attendance-adjusted basic salaryRM 2,700 × 10%RM 270.00
Attendance-adjusted contract earningsRM 3,240 × 10%RM 324.00

Contract earnings include calculated basic salary and contract allowances. In the current implementation, manual earning adjustments are excluded from this percentage basis.

This setting uses the deduction rates stored in the contract; it does not determine statutory tax rates.

4. Use the selected salary divisor for fixed deductions?

This controls how fixed monthly deductions are prorated.

  • Checked: use the divisor selected in Question 1.
  • Unchecked: use calendar-day proration.
Example: A fixed monthly deduction of RM 300. The contract covers 10 calendar days containing 6 scheduled working days. The full month contains 30 calendar days and 22 working days. Question 1 is set to Scheduled working days.
ChoiceCalculationDeduction
CheckedRM 300 × 6 ÷ 22RM 81.82
UncheckedRM 300 × 10 ÷ 30RM 100.00

For a contract covering the full month, both normally produce the full RM 300 deduction. This checkbox controls proration; fixed deductions are not automatically reduced because of an absence.

For deductions based on shorter working time: select Worked hours in Question 2. Choose the remaining rules according to your business’s agreed salary and deduction policy.

Save the policy once in Salary Settings. New calculations and recalculated drafts use the saved rules. Finalized payroll retains its recorded rules and amounts.
